 The Tennis Ball Model 



          To better the communication process, our group created the Table Tennis Communication Model, where the players represent the senders and receivers, the ping-pong ball represents the message, the table represents the channel, the crowd and net represents the noise, and the score represents the effect. This model can improve communication by showing how each element works together during an interaction. Since the sellers and customers take turns sending and receiving messages, the model encourages them to listen carefully and respond appropriately. It also highlights the effects of physical noise, such as loud student conversations, the blender, rain, and other sounds, which may interfere with the message. By recognizing these barriers, the sellers and customers can speak clearly, adjust their volume, pay closer attention, and clarify messages when necessary. Through our model, communication can become more organized and effective, helping prevent misunderstandings and allowing transactions to be completed smoothly.
